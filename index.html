    Narrative description

    A chronological and objective description of events, including officer observations, actions taken, and subject responses.

    1. Initial observation / call for service
    2. Officer response
    3. Interaction with individual(s)
    4. Actions taken
    5. Final disposition

    Time / place / occurrence

    The time and date of occurrence is when the incident happened. The report time is when Public Safety was made aware of the incident — not when the report was written up.

    Example

    On [DATE] at approximately [TIME], Public Safety Officers responded to [LOCATION] for [REASON / OBSERVATION].

    Upon arrival, officers observed [DESCRIPTION OF SITUATION].

    Officers made contact with [INDIVIDUAL NAME / DESCRIPTION] and advised them of [DIRECTIVES / CAMPUS POLICY].

    The individual [COMPLIED / REFUSED / CONTINUED BEHAVIOR]. Officers then [ACTIONS TAKEN – escort, verbal directive, investigation, etc.].

    The individual was [ESCORTED FROM LOCATION / ADVISED / REFERRED / ARRESTED / TURNED OVER TO POLICE].

    No further incidents occurred.

    Remove unnecessary words in sentences.
